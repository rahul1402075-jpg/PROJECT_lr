Future Interest Analysis
in Twitter Users

Predicting future user interests using NLP and Machine Learning

This system studies a user's historical tweets, learns the topics they engage with most, and projects where their attention is likely headed next — Technology today, maybe Education tomorrow. It combines text cleaning, TF-IDF feature extraction, and three classic classifiers (Logistic Regression, Naïve Bayes, SVM) to turn raw tweets into a clear, explainable interest profile.

Upload Dataset Analyze a User View Dashboard
01

Clean & Understand

Every tweet is lowercased, stripped of links/mentions/emojis, tokenized, and lemmatized before it ever reaches a model.

02

Classify & Compare

Logistic Regression, Naïve Bayes, and SVM are trained side-by-side; the best F1-score wins and powers every prediction.

03

Project Forward

Recent vs. older tweet activity is compared to surface a primary future interest and a potential emerging one.
